04 / FAQ

Gemini 4 Carbon pricing answers

How much does Gemini 4 Carbon cost?

On the public Developer API paid tier, input is $2 per million tokens through November 30, 2026 and $4 from December 1, 2026. Output, including thinking tokens, is $10 and then $20. Implicit context caching is $0.10 and then $0.20.

Which date does the calculator use?

Auto resolves today's calendar date in the time zone you select. Explicit uses the date you type and ignores the clock. December 1, 2026 is the first day of the higher schedule. Google does not name a timezone for that change on the pricing page.

Does the free tier cover Carbon?

No. Google's pricing page says Gemini 4 Carbon is not available on the free tier.

How are thinking tokens billed?

The output price includes thinking tokens. Enter thinking separately only when your usage report has not already included those tokens in output.

Is this Google's calculator?

No. LLM Price Calc is an independent summary of published rates. Confirm the official pricing page before you budget a deployment.
