THE SHORT ANSWER

Choose 2.1 for volume and iteration. Choose Pro when maximum fidelity matters.

Nano Banana 2.1 is substantially cheaper at every documented output resolution and is built for efficient generation and conversational editing. Nano Banana Pro remains Google’s specialist choice for high-fidelity work requiring maximum factual accuracy and studio-quality control.

05 / DECISION

Which one should you use?

Use the cheapest model that consistently passes your visual evaluation.

Choose Nano Banana 2.1

  • High-volume asset generation
  • Fast conversational editing and iteration
  • Product variants, localization and social graphics
  • 1K, 2K or 4K output with tight unit economics
  • Workflows needing multiple reference images

Choose Nano Banana Pro

  • Complex compositions where fidelity dominates cost
  • Studio-quality campaign or presentation assets
  • Fact-sensitive diagrams and visual explanations
  • Jobs where failed generations cost more than the price gap
  • Your own evaluation consistently favors Pro

07 / FAQ

Quick answers

Is Nano Banana 2.1 cheaper than Pro?

Yes. Google’s Standard per-image equivalents are $0.0336, $0.0504 and $0.113 for 1K, 2K and 4K Nano Banana 2.1 outputs, compared with $0.134 for 1K/2K and $0.24 for 4K Pro outputs.

Is Nano Banana 2.1 the same as Nano Banana 2?

No. Google describes 2.1 as an update to Nano Banana 2, with improvements to visual quality, prompt adherence, multi-turn consistency and text rendering. Its model code is gemini-nano-banana-2.1.

When should I pay for Pro?

Use Pro when maximum fidelity, complex composition or factual accuracy matters enough to justify the higher price. Always compare both on representative prompts.

Does Nano Banana 2.1 support 4K?

Yes. Google documents 1K, 2K and 4K outputs, with 4K priced at approximately $0.113 per image under Standard processing.

Are there additional costs?

Yes. Input tokens, text and thinking output, and Google Web or Image Search grounding can add charges. The per-image figures above describe image output only.
