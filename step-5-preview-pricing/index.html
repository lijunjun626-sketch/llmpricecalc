04 / BUYING DECISION

Test task economics, not benchmark headlines.

Step 5 Preview may fit

  • You need very long context at a modest token rate
  • Your workflow benefits from tools and structured output
  • You can tolerate preview-model changes
  • You can run an evaluation on real repositories

Choose a stable model instead

  • You require long-term behavior consistency
  • Your compliance process needs a mature SLA
  • You cannot absorb identifier or price changes
  • Your provider region or uptime needs are strict
05 / FAQ

Step 5 Preview pricing answers

How much does Step 5 Preview cost?

OpenRouter currently lists $1 input, $2.70 output and $0.05 cache read per million tokens. Prices are provider-specific and can change.

What is the Step 5 Preview model ID?

Use stepfun/step-5-preview on OpenRouter. StepFun’s first-party API may use step-5-preview; follow the documentation for the endpoint you call.

Does Step 5 Preview support a one-million-token context?

OpenRouter lists a 1,000,000-token context window and up to 64,000 completion tokens.

Is Step 5 Preview good for coding?

It is positioned for agentic work and software engineering, but suitability depends on repository navigation, tool use, patch quality, latency, retries and total tokens on your tasks.

Why does this page say “Preview” so often?

Because the release is not presented as a final stable model. Recheck prices, model identifiers and production behavior before committing a critical workload.
